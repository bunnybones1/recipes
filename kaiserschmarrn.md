# Kaiserschmarrn (Shredded Pancake)

Source: [Ursula, Little Vienna](https://www.lilvienna.com/shredded-pancake-kaiserschmarrn/)

Yield: 2 mains or 4 desserts · Total: 20 minutes

## Equipment

- Ovenproof frying pan, at least 10 inches
- Whisk, mixer, two spatulas

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs, separated
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar, divided (adjust to 1–3 tbsp)
- 200 ml milk (1/2 cup + 1/3 cup)
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract or 1 tsp vanilla sugar
- 125 g all-purpose flour (1 cup / 4.5 oz)
- Optional: 2 tbsp raisins soaked in brown rum
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, ghee, or unsalted butter
- Powdered sugar and plum compote, applesauce, or fruit, to serve

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F / 200°C (fan: 355°F / 180°C).
2. Beat whites, salt, and 1 tbsp sugar to soft-to-firm peaks, avoiding stiffness.
3. Whisk yolks, 1/2 cup milk, vanilla, and remaining sugar. Mix in flour until smooth, then remaining milk.
4. Stir in one-third of whites; gently fold in the remainder.
5. Heat fat over medium without browning butter. Spread batter in pan; scatter raisins. Cook 1 minute.
6. Bake on middle rack 10–12 minutes, until puffed and golden. If still pale at 15 minutes, check underneath for scorching.
7. Return to low stovetop heat. Quarter and flip; brown pale sides over medium if needed. Reduce heat; tear into pieces.
8. Dust with powdered sugar; serve with fruit or compote.
