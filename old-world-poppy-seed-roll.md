# Old World Poppy Seed Roll

Yield: 2 loaves, about 16 servings

Time: 20 mins prep, 30-40 mins bake, about 2 hrs resting/cooling

## Equipment

- food processor
- large mixing bowl
- pastry blender or 2 knives
- rolling pin
- parchment-lined baking sheet
- pastry brush
- clean kitchen towel

## Ingredients

### poppy seed filling

- 1/2 lb poppy seeds
- 3/4 cup white sugar
- 1/2 cup hot milk
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp lemon juice

### bread

- 1/2 cup warm water, about 100f
- 2 tbsp white sugar
- 1 package active dry yeast (0.25 oz, or 2 1/4 tsp)
- 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more as needed
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 large egg, separated

## Instructions

### Prepare the filling

1. Grind the poppy seeds in a food processor for about 1 minute.
2. Mix the ground poppy seeds with sugar, hot milk, melted butter and lemon juice.
3. Cover and refrigerate while you make the dough. It should thicken as it sits.

### Make the dough

1. Add warm water to a small bowl, stir in the sugar, then sprinkle the yeast over top.
2. Let the yeast sit for about 5 minutes, until foamy.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together flour and salt.
4. Cut in the butter until the flour mixture looks like coarse crumbs.
5. Add the yeast mixture and egg yolk, then mix into a soft dough.
6. Knead on a floured surface for about 5 minutes, adding flour 2 tbsp at a time if the dough is too sticky. It should become smooth and a little springy.

### Fill and shape

1. Divide the dough into 2 equal pieces.
2. Roll each piece into a 12x16 inch rectangle.
3. Spread half the filling over each rectangle, leaving about 1 inch clear around the edges.
4. Fold the bare edges over the filling and press them down.
5. Roll each rectangle from a short side, like a jelly roll.
6. Pinch or tuck the ends closed so the filling is less likely to leak.

### Rise and bake

1. Place the rolls seam-side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
2. Let rise somewhere warm until roughly doubled, about 1 hour.
3. Preheat oven to 350f.
4. Beat the reserved egg white until frothy, then brush it over the loaves.
5. Bake for 30-40 minutes, until deep golden brown.
6. Cover the hot loaves with a clean kitchen towel while they cool to keep the crust softer.
7. Cool completely before slicing.
